Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a route offered by mutual funds to invest weekly or monthly rather than in a single lumpsum. It promotes regular savings habits and benefits from rupee cost averaging. When market indexes are volatile, a SIP buys more units at lower NAV values, smoothing out entry prices over time.

The Psychological "Cost of Delay"

In financial compounding, time is more critical than the amount invested. Delaying starting a SIP even by 3 years can cut your final retirement corpus by 30% to 40% due to lost final years of compounded interest. The Delay cost calculator illustrates how a minor delay cost aggregates into a massive deficit in wealth accumulation.
